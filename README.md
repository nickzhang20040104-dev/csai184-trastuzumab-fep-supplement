# BJ-1184 supplementary appendices

Supporting appendices for CSAI 2026 paper BJ-1184, *Evaluating Trastuzumab Affinity Predictions from Alternative Structural Models Using Free Energy Perturbation*.

[Download the bookmarked Appendices A–D PDF](BJ-1184_Appendices_A-D.pdf)

## Appendix A: Additional numerical evidence

PDF pages 2–3. Tables A1 and A2 show per-cycle temporal and block-length sensitivity, checkpoint provenance, and signal-to-noise comparisons. The complete-cycle half differences are supplementary; they were not the edge-leg stopping criterion.

## Appendix B: Prepared structures and prediction confidence

PDF pages 3–9. Tables B1 and B2 summarize structure geometry and native prediction confidence. Figures B1–B4 show the 12 prepared WT and variant models. These are prepared coordinates visualized after repair, not snapshots from terminal trajectories. Predictor-specific confidence metrics are not affinity scores.

## Appendix C: Bennett acceptance ratio analysis

PDF pages 10–11. The appendix describes the two-ensemble estimator and propagation of within-record sampling uncertainty through the thermodynamic cycle.

## Appendix D: Experimental references and data availability

PDF page 12. Table D1 identifies the published affinity point estimates used as the anchor and comparators. The data-availability note describes the scope of this public appendix.

## Data scope

The PDF includes the reported tables and figures at their displayed precision. A separate full-precision source-data archive, postprocessing code, raw energy time series, trajectories, and production run inputs are not included. The appendix supports inspection of the reported evidence but is not a standalone package for rerunning the simulations or independently re-estimating bootstrap uncertainty. The cover lists references cited in the appendices; the numbering follows the manuscript.
